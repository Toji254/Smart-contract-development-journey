ONE CONNECTED PROCESS

Every step has an owner.
Every decision has a history.

Follow a result from submission to release, with the right people responsible at each stage.

01

Submit

The lecturer validates an assessment batch and submits it for review.

02

Review

Department reviewers check completeness and send approved work onward.

03

Reconcile

The exam office checks the record and follows up on missing evidence or exceptions.

04

Approve

Senate-controlled results wait for the required decision; direct-release assessments follow their configured path.

05

Publish

Only results that complete their approval path are made available to students.

The exact release path depends on the institution’s approved assessment policy. MarkTrail does not invent marks, weights or academic outcomes.
